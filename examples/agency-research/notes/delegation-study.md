# Delegation study notes

Participants who delegated routine tasks to an AI agent reported lower perceived agency
after two weeks. The effect was strongest when the agent acted without asking first.
Prepared actions, where the agent drafts and the person approves, kept perceived agency
close to the baseline. Human oversight seems to matter more than the amount of delegation.

Experiment 14 measured perceived agency with a short survey before and after delegation.
Trust calibration improved when the agent explained its reasoning.
